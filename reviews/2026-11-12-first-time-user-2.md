# Preppy: a first-time user's review (2)

Thursday 12 November 2026. I used it mostly at phone size (375 × 812), then looked again at tablet, desktop and in dark mode. I came in through http://localhost:3210/ with storage cleared.

---

## 1. Verdict

I'd keep it on my phone for a fortnight, and I'd decide by the second Sunday. Planning around the days I actually cook makes sense the first time you see it. The shopping list is grouped by aisle and tells you which dish each item is for, and the fridge and freezer split at the end is the kind of help I'd tell a friend about. The one thing to fix first is that it never asks how many people I'm cooking for, and nothing tells me what it assumed. The quantities appear to be for one person: the jollof recipe makes 6 servings with 750 g of rice, but my list asked for 250 g to cover two meals. Anyone cooking for a partner or a family will shop short and only find out on cook day. The second fix is timing. A new user who turns up on a Thursday can't plan anything for this week. Home says "Nothing planned" for today and only offers to plan next week. The best thing about it is how the steps connect: pick cook days, pick dishes, get a list that already leaves out what you have, then see what to put in the fridge and what to freeze.

## 2. The journey as I lived it

- **0:00.** The link opens a landing page. For about a second only the bowl illustration shows, then the headline arrives: "Cook a few times. Eat well all week." I understood what it was within 10 seconds and tapped **Get started**. My first tap did nothing and the second one worked.
- **0:30 to 1:30. Setup, 5 screens.** I picked Sun and Wed as cook days, then foods I avoid (Pork, plus okra typed in), then sides (Rice, Yam, Plantain), then what's in my kitchen (5 items). A screen pitching sign-in came next, then "You're set". That was about 13 taps and I had no result yet.
- **1:30.** **Plan your first cook** took me to "Next week", because this week's cook days had passed.
- **1:45 to 3:30. Sunday cook, 3 steps.** I chose Asaro, Kung Pao Chicken and Party Jollof, with Akara and Pap for breakfast, then checked and saved: "Cook planned. 9 meals from Sunday to Tuesday."
- **About 4:00: first useful result.** I tapped **See shopping list** and got 30 items grouped by aisle, each with the dish it's for. That was about 22 taps from the landing page.
- **4 to 9 min.** I swapped one meal and walked through cook day: "Step 1 of 29", one recipe at a time. It ended on "Portion and store" and **Done cooking**.
- **9 min onwards.** I saved a recipe of my own, planned the Wednesday cook, tried "What can I make?", Settings, Privacy, Sign in, the empty states, reloading, the back button, and the other screen sizes.

## 3. What works, and why

- **The cook-day picker explains itself as you tap.** Choosing Sun and Wed printed "Sunday's cook covers Sunday to Tuesday. Wednesday's covers Wednesday to Saturday." Picking a third day greyed out the rest and said "That's the most." I understood the whole model of the app without reading any help.
- **Each list item names the dish it's for.** The list showed "Garlic 1 bulb · recipes use 4 cloves · Kung Pao Chicken". You buy the right pack size and you know why it's there. Pantry items I'd marked were collapsed under "Already at home 4", with a line explaining how to bring them back.
- **Adding to the list confirms itself.** I typed "toilet paper" and got a button reading **Add "Toilet paper" to your list**. After tapping it, the screen said "Added Toilet paper under Other." The count went from 30 to 31.
- **Swapping a meal suggests the dishes that need the least extra shopping.** Tapping a meal opened a sheet listing other dishes "least extra shopping first", each tagged "Uses 6 fresh things already on your list". That is a real reason to choose one dish over another.
- **"Use it up" on the second cook.** The Wednesday picker opened with "These use fresh food you bought for Sunday's cook" and named the ingredients each dish would use up. That cuts waste, which is the point of meal prep.
- **"What can I make?" is honest about what's missing.** Each result said what I had and what I'd need, for example "Need eggs". It also offered substitutions, like "Use vegetable oil instead of palm oil (changes the taste)". Tapping "Add 3 to my list" a second time said "Those are already on Wednesday's shopping list" instead of adding duplicates.
- **The fridge and freezer step at the end.** For the Wednesday cook it showed: "Fridge (3): Wed lunch, Thu lunch, Fri lunch. Freezer (1): Sat lunch. Move each to the fridge the night before." It's specific and correct, and it's the part other planners leave out.
- **The empty states work.** With nothing planned, the shopping list said "Nothing to buy yet. Plan Sunday's cook and its shopping list fills in" and offered a button. An empty "Read recipe" said "Paste the caption or type a short note first." A search with no results said "Nothing matches. Try another word, or clear the filter."
- **The privacy page is plain and honest.** It says where data lives and that captions are sent to Google's Gemini free tier, which "may use what is sent". It tells you to leave anything personal out, and says screen tracking doesn't record what you type.
- **Screen readers get proper labels.** Plan cells read as "Sunday lunch: Chicken Tikka Masala" and pantry buttons as "Butter: Out. Change to Have". Dish cards in the picker are real toggle buttons.

## 4. What got in my way

Most costly first.

**1. It never asks how many people, and doesn't say what it assumed.** *Stops a new user.*
Where: setup (/welcome), cook builder, shopping list (/shop), recipe page (/library/party-jollof-rice-chicken).
What I did: I planned Party Jollof for 2 meals, then opened the recipe.
Expected: a question about household size, or a visible "for 1 person" somewhere.
What happened: I saw the recipe says "6 servings" with 750 g of rice, and the list asked for "Rice 250 g". Cook day showed "2 portions", which it never defines. Nothing asks for or states a household size. The one-person reading is my inference from those numbers.
Why it matters: for a couple or a family the shop comes up short, and you find out at the stove.
Direction: ask once during setup, and show "for N people" on the cook summary and the list.

**2. A Thursday newcomer can't plan anything this week.** *Stops a new user.*
Where: Home (/), Plan (/plan), "This week".
What I did: I finished setup on a Thursday, then looked at Home and at "Back to this week".
Expected: a way to plan Thursday to Saturday, or at least tonight.
What happened: Home said "Today: Nothing planned" for every meal, plus "No cook days left this week. Plan next week". Tapping "Lunch · Nothing planned" opened next week's plan. "This week" says only "Sunday cook and Wednesday cook have passed." with no action, and tapping a day does nothing. Yet the previous week ("Week of 1 Nov") offers "Plan this cook" for dates that have already gone.
Why it matters: most people try a new app when they hear about it, not on their cook day. The first three days end with nothing.
Direction: offer an extra cook for the days left this week, or let them plan a single meal for today.

**3. Home disagrees with the plan.** *Slows or confuses them.*
Where: Home (/).
What I did: I planned the Sunday 15 Nov cook and marked it cooked, then went Home.
Expected: Home showing the cook as done, or pointing to the Wednesday cook.
What happened: it still said "NEXT COOK Sunday 15 Nov · No cook days left this week · Plan next week".
Why it matters: the screen you open first says you haven't done what you just did.
Direction: base the Next cook card on what has actually been planned and cooked.

**4. The cook summary leaves out a dish I added.** *Slows or confuses them.*
Where: /plan, Sunday cook card.
What I did: I swapped Sunday lunch to Chicken Tikka Masala and tapped Done.
Expected: Tikka Masala in the cook's list of dishes.
What happened: the grid showed it, and cook day included it ("Chicken Tikka Masala · 1 portion"). The card header still listed only Asaro, Kung Pao and Party Jollof.
Why it matters: the summary is how I'd check what I'm cooking. Here it's wrong.

**5. A dietary change doesn't flag meals already planned.** *Slows or confuses them.*
Where: /settings, then /plan.
What I did: I marked "Chicken" under "Never in my food", then looked at the Wednesday cook, which was all chicken.
Expected: a warning on the meals already planned.
What happened: the builder showed a small red "Has chicken" under dishes. It still listed chicken dishes first under "Use it up". The saved Wednesday cook showed no warning at all. The heading says "Never in my food" while the text below says recipes only "get a warning".
Why it matters: someone avoiding an allergen relies on this, and it only works in one place.
Direction: flag meals already planned, and use the same word for "never" and "warning" throughout.

**6. Cook day without a combined order.** I'm judging the fallback here, not the switched-off feature. *Slows or confuses them.*
Where: /plan/cook.
What I did: I followed all 29 steps for the Sunday cook.
What happened:
- Step 1 says "marinate for at least 1 hour, ideally overnight". That's the first I heard of it, on cook day.
- Steps give no amounts ("Mix the Greek yogurt, lemon juice, half the garlic..."). There's no ingredient list per dish and no timer for "Rest 15 min".
- Each step's large heading repeats the first sentence of the step, cut off with "…", and then shows the same sentence in full underneath.
- Reloading at step 3 sent me back to step 1.
- The last Asaro step suggests "Pair it with a protein such as peppered turkey", which isn't in my plan.
Why it matters: this is the screen used with wet hands and a hot pan, and it's the app's whole promise.
Direction: warn about overnight steps when the cook is saved. Show amounts in each step, keep my place after a reload, and drop the repeated heading.

**7. The cuisine labels are wrong in places and change between screens.** *Slows or confuses them.*
Where: the cook picker and the Library filters, compared with the meal swap sheet.
What I saw:
- Ghanaian Red Red is tagged "Nigerian" in the picker and "Ghanaian" in the swap sheet.
- Jamaican-Style Curry Goat is "Indian" in one and "Caribbean" in the other.
- Vietnamese Shaken Beef is "Japanese".
- A five-marinade pack (Korean BBQ, Sweet & Sour, Fajita, Teriyaki, Jamaican Jerk) is tagged "Korean".
Why it matters: the cuisine filters are a main way to browse, and the people most likely to notice are the ones the library is clearly aimed at.

**8. Dishes can't be opened from the picker.** *Slows or confuses them.*
Where: the cook builder's "Choose your mains" (/plan?build=r1) and the meal swap sheet.
What I did: I tried to find out what "Asaro" or "Gizdodo" is before choosing it.
What happened: tapping a card only selects it. There's no info button, and every dish is the same illustrated bowl in a different colour.
Why it matters: I'm choosing three days of food from names and a cuisine tag.
Direction: add a way to look at the recipe from the card without losing your choices.

**9. The "variety" nudge pushes against your own cuisine.** *Small polish.*
Where: the cook builder.
What happened: when I picked two Nigerian mains, a banner appeared: "More than one main is Nigerian. Swap one for variety." Two-thirds of the library is Nigerian. Each banner also pushes the grid down while you're tapping it.

**10. The word for egg count isn't explained.** *Small polish.*
Where: "Check your cook" and the plan card.
What happened: the headline numbers were "0 eggs / 9 meals" and later "3 eggs". After picking Akara, a banner said "No eggs in these." I never said I avoid eggs, and nothing explains why eggs get a headline number.

**11. Two tabs for one job: pantry.** *Slows or confuses them.*
Where: /shop, "At home" and "Stock up".
What happened: At home lists 96 items with 91 marked "Out" by default, and you tap a pill to cycle Out, then Have, then Low. Nothing shows that order. Stock up is a separate "First stock-up 5 of 83" tick list covering much the same items. I couldn't tell which one to use.

**12. Recipes I type in don't merge with the list.** *Small polish.*
Where: /add ("Type it in" tab), then /shop.
What happened: my recipe's "1 onion" and "2 scotch bonnet" appeared in lower case under "Other" instead of joining "Onions" and "Scotch bonnet (rodo)" under Produce. A saved recipe can also only be deleted, not edited. The "Add 3 to my list" button from "What can I make?" added "Bay leaf / Dried thyme / Tomato paste — Added by you" with no amounts and no dish attached.

**13. Smaller things.** *Small polish.*
- Changing servings on a recipe gives "Tomatoes 5¾" and "Seasoning cubes 4⅔".
- "Chicken thighs 4 pieces + 0.4 kg" is a hard thing to ask a butcher for.
- "Plantain as needed" and "Yam as needed" sit on the list for sides.
- The skip button says "Skip, no sweets this cook" on a screen titled "Breakfast and sweets".
- Sweets go by three names: "Sweet" in the plan, "Dessert" on Home, "sweets" in the builder.
- The Plan tab reopened on a past week (Week of 1 Nov) after I'd visited it once.
- Reloading during setup goes back to step 1. The phone's back button leaves setup entirely.
- The inline "Sign in" link on the landing page is about 44 × 18 px, a small tap target.
- In "What can I make?", results appear below about 50 food chips, so ticking things changes nothing you can see.
- There's no dark mode. The page stays bright cream with dark mode switched on.
- I was able to mark the Sunday 15 Nov cook "Cooked" on Thursday 12 Nov.

## 5. What's missing

Ranked.

1. **Household size or servings per cook.** Everyone cooking for more than one person needs it, at their first shop.
2. **A way to plan the rest of this week.** Anyone who starts mid-week needs it, on day one.
3. **Amounts and timers in cook-day steps.** Every cook needs them, on cook day. Mealime and Paprika both show amounts inline and offer timers.
4. **A heads-up for steps that need doing ahead.** Anyone whose recipe needs marinating or soaking needs it the day before the cook.
5. **Prep and cook time on recipes and in the picker.** Anyone deciding whether a cook fits their Sunday needs it while choosing dishes.
6. **Editing a saved recipe.** Anyone who makes a typo or tweaks a recipe needs it straight after saving.
7. **Sharing or exporting the list.** Anyone whose partner does the shop needs it before the shop. Plan to Eat and Samsung Food both have it.
8. **Photos, or at least a way to tell dishes apart.** Anyone who doesn't already know the dish names needs it while choosing dishes.

## 7. Questions a new user would still have

- How many people is this planning for, and can I change it?
- What do I eat tonight, Thursday?
- Is "a portion" one plate?
- Why does it count eggs?
- If I avoid something, will it ever hide those dishes, or only warn me?
- Which pantry screen do I use, At home or Stock up?
- How long will Sunday's cook take, start to finish?
- What do Asaro, Gizdodo or Ayamase look like and taste like?
- How do I fix a typo in a recipe I saved?
- How do I wipe my data if I never sign in? Privacy points to "Settings → Delete my account", which I didn't see while accounts are off.

## 8. Could not test

- **Signing in.** Accounts are switched off. The screens say so.
- **The combined cooking order on cook day.** Switched off. I judged the one-recipe-at-a-time fallback instead.
- **Reading a recipe from a post.** Switched off. I judged the screen and the instructions.
- **Sharing from Instagram or TikTok, and installing to the home screen.** These need a real phone. The browser can't do them.
- **"Settings → Delete my account".** Not visible while accounts are off.
- **The "Recipe by TSpices Kitchen" link.** It goes to another website, so I didn't follow it.
- **Keyboard covering a field on a real phone.** The phone-size browser doesn't show an on-screen keyboard.

## 9. If I could change only five things before a hundred strangers use it

1. Ask how many people during setup, and show it on every list and cook.
2. Let someone who starts mid-week plan the days left, or just tonight.
3. Make Home and the cook summary match what's actually planned and cooked.
4. On cook day, show amounts in each step, warn about overnight steps the day before, and keep my place after a reload.
5. Fix the cuisine labels, and let people open a dish from the picker.

## 10. Go live?

**Yes, after named fixes.** These are what block it:

- No household size, so shopping comes up short for anyone not cooking for one.
- A mid-week starter can't plan anything for this week.
- Home's "Next cook" card contradicts the plan.

## 11. Technical errors seen

- **503 "Service Unavailable" twice** in the browser console during the session. Two cook-day sessions both used the switched-off cooking-order button, so I believe these were that request. That's inference. The request log had dropped those entries by the time I checked.
- **One development warning on the recipe page** (/library/[id]): a note about loading speed during navigation. Nothing visible broke.
- **No other failed requests** in the part of the network log I could see.
