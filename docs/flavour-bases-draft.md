# Flavour bases: draft for review

Seven bases cover 35 of the 44 library dishes. The 8 sweet dishes and Akara stay untagged, and the freezer marinade pack is tagged by its main marinade. Every tag below was checked against the dish's ingredient list. Swaps are labelled **close** (you'd hardly notice) or **changes the taste** (it still works, but it's a different dish).

## The bases

### 1. Pepper base
Tatashe, scotch bonnet and onion, with or without tomatoes, blended and fried down in vegetable oil until the oil rises to the top. Curry, thyme and bay usually go in with it.

- **Sweet body:** tatashe, tomatoes
- **Heat:** scotch bonnet (rodo)
- **Depth:** onion, tomato paste fried until it darkens
- **Fat:** vegetable oil

| Dishes | Swaps |
|---|---|
| Party Jollof, Nigerian Chicken Stew, Spaghetti Jollof, Suya Jollof, Peppered Turkey Wings, Gizdodo, Moi Moi | Tatashe → red bell pepper (close) · Fresh tomatoes → tinned tomatoes (close) · Scotch bonnet → chilli flakes or cayenne (changes the taste: hot but not fruity) |

### 2. Palm oil and iru
Palm oil heated until it clears, then onion, pepper, iru and crayfish. The flavour is earthy and fermented, and the colour is deep red.

- **Fat and colour:** palm oil
- **Fermented depth:** iru (locust beans)
- **Savoury:** ground crayfish, seasoning cubes
- **Heat:** scotch bonnet

| Dishes | Swaps |
|---|---|
| Efo Riro, Egusi Soup, Ayamase (made with green peppers), Asaro, Rich Honey Beans, Red Red | Ugu ↔ spinach (close; squeeze the water out of the spinach) · Palm oil → vegetable oil (changes the taste and colour) · Iru or crayfish left out (changes the taste) |

### 3. Curry and thyme
Curry powder and dried thyme cooked into oil with onion. Lighter and more fragrant than the pepper base.

- **Fragrance:** curry powder, dried thyme
- **Sweet body:** onion, carrots
- **Heat:** scotch bonnet, optional

| Dishes | Swaps |
|---|---|
| Nigerian Fried Rice, Nigerian Chicken Curry Sauce, Jamaican-Style Curry Goat | Dried thyme → fresh thyme (close, use about three times as much) · Frozen mixed vegetables → fresh carrots, peas and green beans (close) |

### 4. Pepper soup spice
Meat simmered in water with pepper soup spice, ginger, garlic and pepper, finished with scent leaf. A clear, peppery broth.

| Dishes | Swaps |
|---|---|
| Goat Meat Pepper Soup | Scent leaf → basil (changes the taste; it's the closest) · Goat → beef or chicken (changes the dish) · Pepper soup spice has no real swap |

### 5. Soy, garlic and ginger
Soy sauce with garlic and ginger, a sweet note (honey or sugar), and often something tart or hot. Cornstarch on the meat makes it glossy and crisp.

- **Salty and savoury:** soy sauce, oyster sauce
- **Sweet:** honey, brown sugar, hoisin
- **Aromatics:** garlic, ginger, spring onion
- **Heat or tang:** gochujang, sriracha, rice vinegar, chillies

| Dishes | Swaps |
|---|---|
| Beijing Beef Bowls, Honey Sesame Fried Rice, Korean BBQ Chicken, Korean Sweet-Chili Beef, Freezer Marinades, Kung Pao Chicken, Crispy Soy Glazed Chicken, Vietnamese Shaken Beef, Pineapple Chicken | Honey ↔ brown sugar (close) · Rice vinegar → white vinegar or lime (close, use less) · Fresh ginger → ground ginger (changes the taste) · Gochujang → sriracha plus a little sugar (changes the taste) · Oyster sauce → hoisin (changes the taste: sweeter) |

### 6. Yogurt and warm spice
Yogurt, lemon and garlic with cumin, coriander, paprika and chilli, used as a marinade or a sauce.

| Dishes | Swaps |
|---|---|
| Chicken Tikka Masala, Chicken Shawarma, Chilli Garlic Chicken Bites | Greek yogurt → plain yogurt (close, thinner) · Lemon ↔ lime (close) · Kashmiri chilli → paprika plus a pinch of cayenne (close) · Garam masala → curry powder (changes the taste) |

### 7. Garlic and paprika rub
A dry rub of garlic powder, paprika and pepper, sometimes finished with a sticky sauce.

| Dishes | Swaps |
|---|---|
| Crispy BBQ & Paprika Chicken, Oven-Baked Beef Ribs, Chicken Fajita Bowls, Philly Cheesesteak, Crispy Fried Chicken, One-Hour Component Meal Prep | Smoked → sweet paprika (changes the taste: no smokiness) · Fajita seasoning → cumin, paprika, garlic and oregano (close) · BBQ sauce → ketchup, honey and smoked paprika (changes the taste) · Provolone → mozzarella (close) |

## Untagged

- **Akara with Pap.** It's only onion and pepper in a bean batter, which doesn't fit any base well.
- **The sweets**, which keep their flavour labels: Banana Bread, Banana Oat Pancake, Brownie Mug Cake, Choco Mousse, Custard Toast, Flan, Nigerian Pancakes, Peanut Butter Banana Oats.

## Cook-day notes this enables

- **Shared base.** When two or more dishes in one cook use the same base, the cook order says to make it once and divide it. This is most useful for the pepper base (jollof plus stew is a common pair) and for palm oil and iru.
- **Crisp parts stay separate.** Fried chicken, crispy soy-glazed chicken, suya, dodo and plantain, and cucumber salads are stored apart from sauces and rice. Crisp things are reheated in the oven or air fryer, never the microwave.

## Calls I made that you may want to change

1. **Suya Jollof goes under Pepper base, not a Suya base of its own.** The rice is a standard jollof; the suya spice is on the beef.
2. **Red Red goes under Palm oil and iru, even though it has no iru.** It's palm oil and pepper, which is closer to that group than to the vegetable-oil pepper base. The same applies to Asaro and Honey Beans.
3. **Pepper soup is a base with only one dish.** I kept it because it's distinctive and saved recipes will add to it. The alternative is to leave the dish untagged.
4. **Pineapple Chicken goes under Soy, garlic and ginger.** It's sweet-sour, but soy is what holds the sauce together.
